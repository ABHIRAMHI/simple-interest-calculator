# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple tool used to calculate the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period at a fixed rate of interest.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

## Inputs
- **Principal:** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate:** The annual rate of interest in percentage.
- **Time:** The duration in years.

## Example
Suppose:
- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate of Interest = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000 = ₹11,000

## Features
- Calculates simple interest.
- Calculates the total amount payable.
- Easy to understand and use.
